I'm Navid Sadeghi, a software engineer and researcher working where AI agents meet cybersecurity. At Amin Raay I build LLM-powered agents that automate security operations, and the SIEM platforms they run on, for both IT and industrial (OT) networks.

My background is in electrical engineering. For my M.Sc. at Shahid Beheshti University I trained reinforcement learning controllers for quadrotors. That background in control systems still shapes how I approach AI: systems that observe, reason, decide and act. Alongside this I run production cloud infrastructure on Kubernetes and AWS EKS.

Beyond the terminal, camping and exploring the outdoors give me room to recharge and see things from a different perspective. I bring that same curiosity, patience, and love of discovery to technology — always learning, building, and exploring what comes next.
